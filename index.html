FE Civil statics formula card

Statics is 8–12 items on FE Civil. Keep the free-body habits sharp; the formulas are short.

Equilibrium

ΣFx=0, ΣFy=0, ΣM=0. Draw the FBD before writing equations. Study unit: equilibrium.

Resultants

R = √(Rx²+Ry²), θ = atan2(Ry,Rx). See resultants and equivalent systems.

Trusses

Method of joints for many members; method of sections when you need 1–2 internals fast. truss analysis · method of sections.

Centroids & friction

Composite centroid x̄ = Σ(Aixi)/ΣAi. Friction F ≤ μN at impending slip. centroids and friction.
